Club AdaBox

Electronics and learning together. Join the club for first dibs on limited-edition AdaBoxes, then buy only the boxes you want.

One annual membership. Boxes sold separately. No fixed drop schedule.

Good hardware takes the time it takes

We loved shipping AdaBoxes every quarter. Parts shortages, shifting tariffs and the scarcity item of the week made that calendar hard to keep. We're still here, building.

Club AdaBox gives us room to make the boxes when the hardware is ready and the projects are right. Your annual membership puts you first in line to hear about each drop and gives you access to the members-only purchase window.

First dibs. Your choice.

When a new AdaBox is ready, we'll email you a link to buy it. Each drop has its own price and purchase window. You choose whether to buy or skip it.

Your membership pays for access, not a shipment of boxes. AdaBoxes are limited runs, so membership does not reserve a box or guarantee stock.

How it works

1. Join the club

Sign up for a year of Club AdaBox membership. Your membership renews each year unless you cancel renewal.

2. Watch for the drop

We'll email you when a new AdaBox is ready, with its price, purchase window and a link to the product page.

3. Buy only the boxes you want

Sign in with the account you used to join, then buy the box during the member window while stock lasts. Not your kind of project this time? Skip it and watch for the next one.

Frequently asked questions

Does my membership include the physical AdaBoxes?

No. Membership gives you first access to buy new AdaBoxes. Each box is a separate purchase, and you can buy or skip each drop.

How often will new AdaBoxes drop?

There's no fixed schedule. We build boxes when the hardware is ready and the projects are right. Sometimes that's a few months, sometimes longer. Members hear first.

Do I have to buy every AdaBox?

No. Your membership gives you the option, not the obligation. Buy only the boxes you want.

Is Club AdaBox available internationally?

Club AdaBox is available in the United States at launch. We'll share updates here if that changes.

How long do I have to buy a box?

Each drop has a members-only purchase window. We'll announce the window with the drop. Stock is limited, and a purchase window is not a reservation.

Can non-members buy an AdaBox?

Members get first dibs. After the member window closes, any remaining boxes may be offered to everyone.

I was an AdaBox Classic subscriber. What happens to my old subscription or gift?

Club AdaBox is a separate annual membership. For questions about your AdaBox Classic subscription, gift or prepaid boxes, email [email protected] so we can look at your account.

Does my membership renew automatically?

Yes, once a year unless you cancel renewal. You can check your renewal date and manage your membership at your membership page.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

You can cancel within 14 days of signing up for a full refund of your membership fee. Your membership access ends when you cancel with a refund. After 14 days, the membership fee is non-refundable for the current term. You can cancel renewal and keep your access through the expiration date shown on your membership page.

If you cancel with a refund and later want to join again, contact [email protected]. Rejoining after a refunded cancellation needs help from our team.

Is there a box available now?

Check the Club AdaBox page in the shop. If no boxes are available, watch your inbox for the next drop.
